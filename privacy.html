PGPost Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

PGPost collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We have no servers that receive anything from the app.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your keys are stored in the iPhone Keychain, marked “this device only”. They are not synced to iCloud Keychain and are not included in iCloud or computer backups.
  • Saved unlocks. If you choose “Remember with Face ID”, the unlocked key is kept in the Keychain behind Face ID or Touch ID. iOS will not release it without a biometric match, it is never backed up, and iOS deletes it if you remove your passcode.
  • Messages and files you encrypt, decrypt, sign or verify are processed entirely on the device. Temporary files are cleared the next time the app starts.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Only what you choose to share. Encrypted messages, public keys and backups leave the phone only when you copy, share or save them.
  • Finding a key by email (PGPost Pro). When you tap Search, the email address you typed is sent to that address’s mail domain (Web Key Directory) and to keys.openpgp.org to look up a public key. Nothing is looked up in the background.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Clipboard

PGPost checks only whether the clipboard contains text, and reads it only when you tap Paste.

Children

PGPost does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
